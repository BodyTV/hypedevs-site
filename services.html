    Questions

    Before you commit.

    What if I only have a rough idea?
    That’s normal. Tell us the problem you want solved and we’ll suggest a scope, a price and the simplest way to get there.
    Can you work with my existing code?
    Often yes. Send us the project and we’ll tell you honestly whether extending it is cheaper than rebuilding.
    Do you host it for me?
    We can guide you to a host, or set it up directly. Hosting costs are separate from the build price.
    What if I need changes later?
    Small tweaks are usually free right after delivery. Bigger additions are quoted as a new piece of work.

    Not sure which service you need?

    Describe the problem and we’ll point you to the right one — even if that means a smaller price tag.
