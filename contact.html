How soon will you reply?
Most messages get an answer within a few hours, and almost always inside two days. Discord is the fastest way to reach us.
What should I include in my first message?
A sentence about what you want built, who it’s for, and any examples you like. If you used the estimator, paste the summary in too.
Do you take small projects?
Yes. Skripts and smaller bots are some of our favourite work — starting at €30.

We keep a small queue so every project gets proper attention. Ask and we’ll tell you the current wait honestly.
