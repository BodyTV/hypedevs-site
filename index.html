    Questions

    Good to know.

    How do we start?
    Message us on Discord with a short description of your idea. We’ll reply with questions, a rough price and a timeline — usually within two days.
    How long does a project take?
    Most bots and plugins take between three and ten days depending on complexity. We show you progress along the way so there are no surprises.
    Do you offer support after launch?
    Yes. Every delivery includes a working handover, and we help with fixes and small updates after launch. Larger changes are quoted separately.
    What do you need from me?
    Just the idea and a few details: who it’s for, what it should do, and any examples you like. We handle the technical decisions.
    How does payment work?
    Half up front to reserve your slot, half when you’re happy with the delivery. Prices shown are starting points for the simplest version.

    Ready when you are.

    Tell us what you’re building. If we’re the right fit, we’ll say so — and if we’re not, we’ll point you somewhere better.
